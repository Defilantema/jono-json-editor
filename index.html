Advanced placement & optimisation

The preview compares horizontal, vertical and largest-first rectangle packing and uses the smallest result. It preserves visible pixels exactly but does not guarantee a mathematically minimal rectangle count.

Simplified uses compact block letters and clock-style digits (A–Z, 0–9, common punctuation). Lowercase is rendered as uppercase. Other characters fall back to a browser font. Change Pixel size to scale the art.
Supports letters, digits, punctuation and scripts supported by your browser's installed fonts. If a character displays as a missing-glyph square in the preview, choose another font or use a local font with that script. All text exports as ordinary wall entities; no fonts are needed in Jono.

Shortcuts & tips

Tools

V Select / move · B Brush · E Eraser · L Line · R Rectangle · F Fill · D Exit · S Spawn · H Pan · A Area

View

Hold Space and drag to pan. Use the wheel to zoom (change this in Settings). Esc clears the selection.

Precision

Shift+drag snaps to 1/256 tile, Alt+drag to 1/8, Ctrl+drag to 1/2. Keys 1–7 choose the move step.

History

Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z redo · ? this help

Time

Jono runs at 60 ticks per second. A period of 120 means 2 seconds from A to B.

Area tool

Drag a rectangle to grab every tile, object and sign inside it. Drag inside it, type an exact move, or use the arrow keys to shift it. Crop makes the map exactly that area, which helps trim big stitched maps to 40,000 tiles.

Map edges

With Select or Area, drag any edge or corner of the map to trim or extend that side. The label shows the new size and tile count. Trimming the left or top moves everything with it.

Triggers

Objects with the same trigger number activate together. needs makes a trigger wait for another one. Locks use the key's trigger number.

Level-check labels: Problem means Jono is known or expected to reject it. Limit means it is outside a limit documented in the community schema. Submission only matters when you submit. Note is information. Each item names its source.
